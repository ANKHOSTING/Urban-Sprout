**Full description:**

Grow more in small spaces. Urban Sprout is your pocket companion for balcony, windowsill, rooftop, and indoor gardens.

🪴 MY PLANTS
Add your plants and get care info for favorites like basil, tomatoes, pothos, and succulents. Set watering schedules and get friendly reminders so nothing goes thirsty.

💬 ASK AI
Yellow leaves? Tiny bugs? Not sure what grows on a shady balcony? Ask our AI plant expert and get clear, practical advice in seconds.

📓 GARDEN JOURNAL
Snap photos and jot notes to track sprouts, harvests, and what worked, season after season.

🧺 COMMUNITY MARKET
Share cuttings, seeds, and homegrown produce with local gardeners. Sell, swap, or give away. Report and block tools keep the community friendly.

🌟 SPROUT PRO
Upgrade for unlimited AI advice and an ad-free experience. Free users get 5 AI questions a day.

Whether you're keeping one herb alive or running a rooftop jungle, Urban Sprout helps your city garden thrive.
